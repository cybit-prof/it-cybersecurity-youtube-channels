# IT-cybersecurity-YouTube-channels
The following are the best YouTube channels on IT and cybersecurity topics:  
**Certifications & Foundations**  
[Professor Messer](https://www.youtube.com/professormesser): Professor Messer provides structured, free training courses for CompTIA certifications like A+, Network+, and Security+.  

[CertMike](https://www.youtube.com/@CertMike/videos): Mike Chappel focuses almost entirely on cybersecurity education and professional IT certification preparation.  

**Ethical Hacking & Pentesting**  
[John Hammond](https://www.youtube.com/@_JohnHammond): John Hammond teaches malware analysis, CTF (Capture The Flag) challenges, and reverse engineering.  

[LiveOverflow](https://www.youtube.com/@LiveOverflow): Provides content on various IT security topics and participating in hacking competitions.  

[Hak5](https://www.youtube.com/@hak5): Provides educational content focused on cybersecurity, penetration testing (ethical hacking), network infrastructure, and hacker culture.  

[Black Hills Information Security](https://www.youtube.com/@BlackHillsInformationSecurity): Features cybersecurity educational content, live webcasts, and technical training tutorials.  

[NahamSec](https://www.youtube.com/nahamsec): Ben Sadeghipour provides live recon streams, bug-bounty methodology, interviews with top hunters.  



**Networking & Automation**  
[David Bombal](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCP7WmQ_U4GB3K51Od9QvM0w): David Bombal covers network engineering, Python automation, info security, pentesting, hacking, and interviews with industry experts.  

[NetworkChuck](https://www.youtube.com/@NetworkChuck): Provides technology and information technology (IT) education content focused on career building, IT certifications, programming, cybersecurity, and hands-on labs.   


**Career Strategy & Governance**  
[Simply Cyber](https://www.youtube.com/@simplycyber): Hosted by Gerald Auger, PhD, brings Information security related content to enable people to have a rewarding cybersecurity career.  Focuses on career transitions, soft skills, and GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance).  

[UnixGuy|Cybersecurity](https://www.youtube.com/@UnixGuy): Provides career-focused content, training, and advice designed to help people land jobs in the cybersecurity industry.
